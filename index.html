I'm a Class 10 student who kept losing hours jumping between a chat tab, a notes app, and a YouTube search that returned twenty videos and one good one. So I built the page I wanted: ask, write, watch, keep going.

It's a single HTML file. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and your notes stay on your own machine. If it saves you one evening before a test, it did its job.
